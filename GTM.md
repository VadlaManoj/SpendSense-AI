# Go-To-Market Strategy

**Target Persona**: Engineering Managers and Founders at seed to Series B startups with 5-50 engineers. These teams often adopt AI tools bottom-up (developers swiping credit cards), leading to fragmented, unoptimized spend.

**Acquisition Channels**:
1. **Engineering Influencers**: Partner with tech Twitter/LinkedIn creators to share "How we cut our AI spend by 30%".
2. **Product Hunt Launch**: Launch "AI Spend Audit by Credex" as a free tool for the community.
3. **SEO**: Capture long-tail keywords like "Cursor vs Copilot enterprise pricing" or "ChatGPT team vs enterprise cost".

**First 100 Users**: Direct outreach to YC and 500 Startups founders in our existing network. Offer a personalized "white-glove" audit review in exchange for using the tool.

**Unfair Distribution**: Leveraging the Credex brand. We already provide credits for AWS/GCP, so we have a natural list of startups looking to save money. We can embed this tool into our existing onboarding flow.

**Traction Metrics**:
- Number of audits completed per week.
- Total savings identified (cumulative).
- Lead conversion rate (Audits completed -> Email submitted).
