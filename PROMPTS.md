# Prompts

## Audit Summary Generation Prompt

**Prompt Text:**
> You are an expert cloud and AI infrastructure financial analyst.
> Review the following AI spend audit for a startup:
> [INSERT AUDIT DATA JSON]
> Write a 100-word personalized summary explaining their current setup, where they are overspending, and the primary benefit of the recommended changes. Be encouraging, professional, and concise. Highlight that Credex can help optimize this further if applicable.

**Why chosen:**
This prompt is designed to produce a professional, actionable summary that reinforces the value of the audit and creates a smooth transition to the Credex CTA. It enforces a strict word limit to keep the UI clean.

**Failed prompt experiments:**
- "Summarize this data." -> Too generic, robotic, and ignored the business context.
- "Write a sales pitch for Credex based on this data." -> Too aggressive, alienated users who just wanted a helpful audit.
