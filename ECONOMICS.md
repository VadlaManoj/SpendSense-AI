# Unit Economics & Monetization

**Lead Value**: For Credex, a high-intent startup founder looking to save on cloud/AI spend is worth approximately $1,500 in lifetime value (LTV), assuming they adopt our credit infrastructure and route their billing through us.

**CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**: 
- Product Hunt / Twitter organic: $0
- Paid Social (LinkedIn/Twitter): targeting $150 per qualified lead (email + >$500/mo in savings identified).

**Conversion Funnel**:
1. 1000 Landing Page Visitors
2. 200 Audits Completed (20%)
3. 40 Emails Submitted (20% of audits)
4. 10 Qualified Calls Booked (25% of emails)
5. 2 Customers Closed (20% close rate)
Value created: 2 * $1,500 = $3,000. 
Spend: $150 * 10 = $1,500. 
LTV:CAC ratio = 2:1 (Healthy for early stage, target is 3:1 at scale).

**$1M ARR Path**: 
To hit $1M ARR ($83k/mo), assuming $12k ACV per customer, we need ~7 new customers a month.
At our funnel metrics, we need to generate ~3,500 landing page visitors per month and identify significant savings for at least 700 of them. This is highly achievable with organic SEO growth over 12 months.
